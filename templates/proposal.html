YOUR FIRST DESIGN EXERCISE

Harborview Medical: secure patient portal

A medical group needs a patient portal that stays available during an Availability Zone failure. Patient records must remain private, encrypted, monitored, and backed up.

1. ExploreLoad the guided scenario and inspect the 12 AWS services, 6 subnets, and existing data flows.
2. Understand placementPublic subnets host the load balancer; application and database resources belong in private subnets.
3. ExperimentAdd another service from the catalog, move a resource card, or create a test subnet.
4. VerifyReload the design. Your infrastructure, connections, and canvas positions should remain saved.

Important: This is an educational architecture scenario. It does not configure a live AWS account or establish production security.
